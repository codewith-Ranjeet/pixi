# Pixi UltraChat SFT data provenance

- Source: https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceH4/ultrachat_200k
- Immutable dataset revision: `8049631c405ae6576f93f445c6b8166f76f5505a`
- Dataset card license: MIT. The card describes the dialogues as generated by ChatGPT; inspect samples and applicable source terms before redistribution.
- Official splits used: `train_sft` for training and `test_sft` for validation; no split mixing.
- Filtering: alternating user/assistant turns, non-empty English text as supplied by the source, complete turn packing within 512 tokenizer tokens, deduplicated conversations; long first answers are token-truncated to fit.
- System prompt: `You are Pixi, a friendly and helpful English AI assistant.`
- Prepared counts: 10000 train, 1000 validation.
- Tokenizer SHA-256: `aa083f69cc3ec2ff579ef815f1bf867c352e3cf76dc869b4cc6679a0e31c7aff`
- Sampling seed: 2026; maximums: 10000 train, 1000 validation.
- Rebuild with `python scripts/prepare_ultrachat_sft.py`.
